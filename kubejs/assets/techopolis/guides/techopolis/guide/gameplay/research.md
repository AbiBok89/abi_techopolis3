# Research

After obtaining Basic Technium, you can create Basic Research. Inside the Quest Book, you can see the Tech Tree questline, which shows the Research you can unlock.

<ItemImage id="techopolis:basic_research" />

## Researcher

The Researcher is used to create various types of Research: Basic, Advanced and more. These Researches can then be used to create Blueprints.

<ItemImage id="techopolis:researcher" />

## Research Crafter

The Research Crafter uses Blueprints to create useful items. Some recipes may also require Energy and Fluids.

<ItemImage id="techopolis:research_crafter" />
