# Grass Block

## Honey, I lost my Grass Block

Sometimes you may need vanilla Grass Blocks. If you have used up your Grass Blocks, for example on a Skyblock, this is how you get them back.

### No Wheat Seeds

* Use a Stonecutter to get Colored Grass.
* Use Bone Meal on the Colored Grass to get Wheat Seeds or Colored Flowers.
* Use Wheat Seeds on a block of Dirt to create a Grass Block.
