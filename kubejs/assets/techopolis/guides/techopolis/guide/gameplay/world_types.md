# World Types

Techopolis 3 can be played in a few different world types. Some of these world types contain additional recipes, which are automatically enabled or disabled depending on the selected world type.

## Normal

The default world type. Vanilla-like generation, with some structures removed and loot chests nerfed. Ores are useless.

## Hex Lands

A world generated with hexes.

This has two different options:

* **Hexlands Overworld** enables hexland generation in the Overworld.
* **Hexlands** enables this generation in The Nether as well.

## Super Flat

A vanilla-like Super Flat world. The default Super Flat has had its amount of dirt increased by 60 blocks, making it easy to build in. Contains a few extra recipes and mechanics.

## Skyblock

Just the island and you. No other structures spawn in this world type, leaving lots of space to build. Contains a few extra recipes and mechanics.

## Resource Curio

<ItemImage id="techopolis:resource_curio" />

In Skyblock and Super Flat, a Resource Curio, when equipped, allows you to obtain additional items from Dirt and Grass blocks respectively.
